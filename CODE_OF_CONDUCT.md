# Code of conduct

LR2021-SDR welcomes people who want to build, measure, test, document or learn about the project. Treat one another with respect, regardless of experience, background, identity or ability.

## Working together

- Keep feedback about the work. Explain the problem and give others room to respond.
- Be patient with questions and honest about what you have tested.
- Respect people's time, privacy and boundaries.
- Accept corrections and help keep discussions useful.

Harassment, threats, slurs, sexual attention, personal attacks and sharing someone's private information without permission are not acceptable. Repeated disruption or unwanted contact is also grounds for removal.

This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions and other project spaces, and when representing the project elsewhere.

## Reporting a problem

For a public moderation concern, flag the relevant discussion for the maintainer, [SAMS0N1TE](https://github.com/SAMS0N1TE). Keep personal or sensitive details out of public posts. To report abuse privately to GitHub, use the **Report content** option on the offending content or [GitHub's abuse report form](https://github.com/contact/report-abuse).

Security vulnerabilities have a separate [private reporting process](SECURITY.md).

## Moderation

The maintainer may edit or remove content, lock a discussion, issue a warning, or temporarily or permanently block participation. The response depends on the severity and whether the behaviour is repeated. Serious threats or harassment may lead to an immediate block.

Reports should be handled with care. Personal information should only be shared when needed to address the report.
